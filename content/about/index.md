# About Me

Hi! I'm **Felix Billières**, and I do CTFs and research under the pseudonym **Elliot Belt**.

## My Journey

I did one year of SOC N1/N2, then one year of pentest, and I'm currently a Purple Teamer in internship. My role involves identifying vulnerabilities while improving system security measures.

## My Passions

### Active Directory
Passionate about Active Directory and its complex mechanisms. I do a lot of HTB boxes, create offensive tools, collaborate on open-source projects, and develop cool Active Directory tools.

### Web Pentesting / Bug Bounty
In the Phreaks 2600 team, I mainly focus on web security, lead web workshops for players, and do bug bounty on YesWeHack, Yogosha, and HackerOne.

### Creating Offensive and Defensive Tools
I create tools mainly related to Active Directory, as well as tools that help with purple teaming (not yet public). I had the opportunity to present my biggest project - a tool called AuditMapper - at a conference: WineRump in Bordeaux.

### CTF (Capture The Flag)
I actively participate in CTFs with the **Phreaks 2600** team. These competitions allow me to develop my skills in a fun and collaborative way. You can follow my progress on [Root-Me](https://www.root-me.org/Elliot_Belt).

### Purple Teaming
My internship as a Purple Teamer allows me to:
- Conduct penetration tests
- Analyze attacker tactics
- Improve organizational defenses
- Collaborate with red and blue teams
